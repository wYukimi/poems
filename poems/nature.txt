Peaceful Nature,
Dew in the winter's morning,
The plains remain pearly white.
Nothing's purer;
This air where mysteries are floating,
This breeze, seeming light
Almost freezing, fragrant,
Soft, and making him
Fall asleep and dream in the present
Gazing up at the sky and grin...
Nature unusually calming,
I would go wandering.

Calm Nature,
The atmosphere is so pleasing,
An eternity we could spend with ease.
But beneath this verdant ceiling,
In the depths of what time might seize,
Serenity is hard to appease.
Life's horrors endure, unyielding,
He lies amidst the ox-eye blooms;
And with a touch of careless feeling,
His heart finds calm within its gloom.

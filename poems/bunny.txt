By a sparkling winter snow,
Counting neither hop nor go,
The bunny leaps with joy and glee.
Across the pure and bright meadow,
Marked in time by an upcoming sorrow,
He ventures forth, both brave and free.
Startled as if by sudden shot,
The rabbit jumps, full of fright.
He hides beneath the flowers, distraught;
The snow whispers, soft and light
Oh Rabbit, in this wintry sky,
Take in the beauty as you try.

On a dazzling, pearly snow,
Beneath the skies of azure delight,
The rabbit gazes at the scene.
He daydreams, wandering in glow
Through this canvas, calm and white,
Before he spies a human mien.
Startled as if by sudden shot,
Under the sky remaining a vivid blue
In the frosty, icy blot,
The snow now tinges with crimson hue.
